Assumptions

A quick business case for a new site: move the sliders, read the bill. It is a 5 minute demo to show the kind of tools I can build, not a real analysis. Almost every number is made up, except these few found online:

  • UK sales of £56.2m in 2025, up 27%, gross margin 75% (Companies House, via Restaurant Online)
  • Average spend of roughly £27 to £60 across the estate (Victor Lugger, MCA conference, 2023)
  • Edmondo: 200 covers and three private dining rooms near Bank, opening 6 November 2026

How is a new site doing, week by week?

A weekly dashboard to follow a restaurant once it is open: covers, spend, team cost, profit and private rooms. Click a week in the chart to see its numbers day by day.

  • Example: a 200 cover City site, first 14 weeks after a 6 November opening. All data is invented.
